# Codebase to Course: demo script

Speaker script for the client demo of the **codebase-to-course** AI skill.
Length: about 4–5 minutes including the demo. Priya hands over before this part.

---

## 1. Handoff and intro (about 15 seconds)

> Thanks, Priya.
>
> In this part, I'll walk you through one of our AI use cases: turning a codebase into a course. First, I'll explain the problem, and then how we're solving it with the help of AI.

## 2. The problem (about 45 seconds)

> When a new joiner comes into a team, we usually do KT sessions, where a senior walks them through the codebase.
>
> KT sessions are a great way for a senior to share their knowledge of the codebase. But a codebase is a lot to take in at once, and it's hard to measure how much of it has really been absorbed. Naturally, a new joiner comes back with follow-up questions, and it takes a few iterations to get fully comfortable with the codebase. So we asked ourselves: how can we make this process even smoother for both the senior and the new joiner?

## 3. The AI solution (about 45 seconds)

> So we built an AI skill to support this. You point it at a codebase, it scans the whole thing, and it generates a structured course: an overview of the architecture, the key modules, how the main flows work.
>
> And at the end of each section, it creates quiz questions. So the new joiner can learn at their own pace, go back to any module whenever they need to, and test themselves as they go. The quiz shows them right away which areas they've understood well and which ones need a bit more focus. So when they do reach out to the senior, they come with specific, focused questions, which makes those conversations much more productive.
>
> Let me show you.

## 4. Demo (about 2.5 minutes)

1. Show the codebase briefly, so the client sees it's real.
2. Show how the skill is triggered (one command or prompt).
3. Switch to a course that was already generated. Don't wait for a live scan.
4. Click through one or two modules.
5. Answer one quiz question, getting one wrong on purpose. Say:
   *"See, now I know exactly which module to revisit."*

## 5. Closing and handoff (about 20 seconds)

> So along with the KT session, the new joiner now has a self-paced course they can revisit anytime, and a simple way to check their understanding. It makes onboarding smoother for everyone.
>
> That's it from my side. Thank you. Over to you, Priya.

---

## Tone notes

- Keep it positive: KT sessions and seniors are valuable. The AI course **supports** KT, it doesn't replace it.
- Avoid words like "one-way", "slow", "expensive" or "instead of KT".
- If asked about accuracy: the senior reviews the generated course, so they move from explaining everything to reviewing it.
- Pause briefly after "Thanks, Priya" and nod to Priya on the final handoff.
