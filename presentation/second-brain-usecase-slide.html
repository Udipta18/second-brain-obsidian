Use Case: Copilot with a second brain in Obsidian

WITHOUT AI
Developer re-explains the ticket and repo in every new chat
Past fixes and decisions are lost when the chat closes
Knowledge scattered across Jira, Confluence, Teams and heads
WITH AI
Jira ticket or Confluence page pasted with /capture
Skill writes linked notes into the Obsidian vault
/recall reads the notes and briefs the chat before work starts
/log saves work and decisions, /connect links related notes weekly
OBSIDIAN VAULT
Tickets
Projects
Knowledge
Decisions
Daily notes
Memory that survives every chat
Notes linked like neurons
Never runs commands

Without AI: every Copilot chat starts from zero, so context is re-explained and decisions are lost. With AI: tickets and knowledge become linked notes in an Obsidian vault, Copilot recalls them before a task and logs the work after it, and the graph view shows how everything connects.
